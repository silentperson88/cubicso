# Milestone Update

Our Heavy Engineering program teams reached a major execution milestone by completing **39,542 meters of piling** across multi-location packages. The work was executed under a common governance model that aligned client inspections, field quality checks, and daily production planning.

## What Was Delivered

- End-to-end piling package execution including layout checks, boring, cage placement, and concreting.
- Staged mobilization of rigs and support equipment to reduce idle cycles at site.
- Quality checkpoint system for reinforcement, verticality, concreting records, and pour traceability.
- Integration with client review cadence for faster sign-off and lower rework risk.

## Execution Controls Used on Site

1. Daily micro-planning by zone and rig sequence.
2. Safety pre-task review before each shift.
3. Real-time progress logging against weekly targets.
4. Escalation matrix for material, manpower, and weather risks.

## Project Impact

The program delivered better schedule confidence for downstream civil activities and improved interface readiness for RCC and structural teams. It also reduced stoppage events through early coordination between construction, QA/QC, and client representatives.

## Next Focus

Cubicso teams are now progressing linked heavy civil fronts including roadwork tie-ins, structural foundations, and high-volume concrete interfaces with the same safety-time-quality model.
