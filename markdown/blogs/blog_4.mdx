# Site Engineering Bulletin

Floating solar projects demand stronger early-stage controls than conventional installations. Cubicso teams advanced readiness by integrating geological review, anchoring logic, and construction planning before full mobilization.

## Workstreams Completed

- Site and boundary condition assessment for floating infrastructure compatibility.
- Engineering validation of support systems and installation sequence.
- Risk scenario planning for weather windows, access, and logistics.
- Construction method statements aligned with safety and quality requirements.

## Risk Controls Introduced

- Stage-gate approval before moving from design to procurement.
- Technical review board for changes affecting safety or schedule.
- Single-source reporting format for design, procurement, and execution teams.

## Program Benefit

By front-loading technical diligence, the team reduced downstream disruption and improved confidence in commissioning timelines.

## What Comes Next

Execution teams are aligning procurement and field deployment so installation starts with full visibility on critical path dependencies.
