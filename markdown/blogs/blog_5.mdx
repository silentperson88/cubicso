# Wealth Management Update

Cubicso Wealth Management teams launched an AI-powered equity desk experience that combines market data, signal generation, and advisor-facing insights in a single operational view.

## Capability Rollout

- AI-assisted stock screening using trend, volatility, and momentum indicators.
- Live market dashboard with sector-level and portfolio-level movement tracking.
- Portfolio health indicators for diversification, drawdown, and concentration alerts.
- Advisor review workspace with standardized consultation notes.

## Operational Improvements

1. Faster signal interpretation for intraday and swing decision windows.
2. Better risk communication between analysts and advisory teams.
3. Structured review workflows for client portfolio adjustments.

## Compliance and Governance

Advisory workflows were designed around SEBI-aligned consultation practices, auditability, and controlled recommendation communication.

## Client Value

The platform supports more informed, timely, and transparent decisions for growth-oriented and risk-managed portfolios.
