# Operations Intelligence Note

Cubicso deployed a smart robotics dashboard that consolidates machine performance, downtime events, and maintenance readiness into a single operational cockpit.

## Dashboard Coverage

- OEE drivers by line, shift, and work cell.
- Fault trend analysis and recurring stop-cause mapping.
- Maintenance planning panel with task prioritization.
- Alert layer for threshold breaches and response ownership.

## Field Benefits

- Faster identification of bottlenecks and repeat failures.
- Better maintenance scheduling without production surprises.
- Clear accountability across production, automation, and maintenance teams.

## Why This Matters

In industrial automation environments, speed without visibility increases risk. This dashboard improves decision quality while protecting schedule reliability.

## Roadmap

Next releases will include predictive warning logic and cross-line benchmarking to support continuous productivity gains.
