# Fintech Operations Note

Cubicso implemented a combined news-intelligence and advisor workflow layer that improves how market events are interpreted, prioritized, and converted into client actions.

## What Was Integrated

- Curated financial news feed mapped by sector and portfolio relevance.
- Event tagging engine for policy changes, earnings impact, and macro signals.
- Advisor task queue with response SLAs and decision checklists.
- Unified view connecting market event, portfolio impact, and action plan.

## Measurable Improvement Areas

- Reduced lag between market trigger and advisor guidance.
- Improved consistency in client communication during volatility.
- Better prioritization of portfolios with higher risk sensitivity.

## Enterprise Environment

The system is deployed in a professional corporate operating setup with role-based access and review workflows to maintain execution integrity.

## Next Iteration

Teams are extending intelligent portfolio analytics with scenario simulation modules for advisory planning.
