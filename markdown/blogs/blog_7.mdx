# Automation Deployment Update

Cubicso Robotics teams completed a production-cell pilot for repetitive industrial operations, integrating robotic handling, programmable motion logic, and quality verification points.

## Pilot Scope

- Automation of repetitive handling and positioning tasks.
- Sensor-driven checks for sequence confirmation and tolerance control.
- Integration with existing line operations to reduce disruption.
- Operator enablement plan for handover and ongoing supervision.

## Performance Indicators

- Increased cycle stability and reduced unplanned stoppages.
- Lowered manual intervention on high-frequency tasks.
- Improved consistency of output against quality thresholds.

## Safety and Reliability

Risk assessments were completed for motion zones, operator interaction points, and emergency response logic before production rollout.

## Program Expansion

The pilot framework is now being adapted for additional cells with higher complexity and mixed-task automation requirements.
